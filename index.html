Practice the moment.
Prove it in the numbers.

Cue finds the skill a team is missing in real work, turns it into a 10 minute practice with an AI avatar, and shows if it moved the numbers.

01 Problem

Learning platforms count views, not wins.

Today's LXP is a library with a feed. People watch, mark it done, and go back to work. Nobody can say if anything changed.

  • Far from work. Learning lives in another tab, not where the deal happens.
  • Watching is not doing. There is no practice before the real moment.
  • No proof. Reports show hours and completions, not results.
02 Insight

The manager is the lever.

Frontline managers turn learning into results. They need to see the gap, act in minutes, and know if it worked.

Three people, one loop
  • Noa, sales team leadShow me where my team loses, and help me fix it fast.
  • Sam, account executiveHelp me nail Thursday's call without losing my week.
  • Ira, head of enablementShow me which practice moves the numbers, so I can scale it.
Who we design for

Teams whose conversations make or lose money. Sales first, then support, success and new managers.

Why Kaltura

Kaltura already owns video and real-time AI avatars. Cue turns them into a weekly habit tied to a business number.

03 Solution

One loop, five moves.

A manager spots a gap, Cue builds a practice, the team rehearses with an avatar, and everyone sees what changed.

04 Results

Measure what changes, not what's watched.

Cue checks behavior in weeks and revenue in quarters, and compares people who practiced with people who haven't yet.

What we measure, by when
  • Weeks. Behavior on real calls. Value before price, fewer discounts.
  • A month. Win rate at the hard step.
  • A quarter. Revenue per person, and time to first deal for new people.
Guardrails
  • People feel watched. Practice rounds are private until you share one.
  • Scores feel unfair. Anyone can flag a score.
  • Did practice cause it? Every number is compared with people who haven't practiced yet, and labeled with how sure we are.
How it grows

The same loop works for support (an angry customer), customer success (a renewal talk), new managers (a hard feedback talk) and healthcare (hard news).

What's next

Live hints during practice, practice inside Zoom right before a call, and templates that improve themselves from what works.
